02 / From browsing to a useful shortlist

How to use a PikoBuy spreadsheet.

A spreadsheet organizes product research. Its value comes from the evidence saved beside each link.

01 / IDENTIFY

Save the exact product option

Record the source URL, seller, color, size, quantity and date checked. A listing may offer several variants at different prices. Open the source again before selecting an option; a saved photograph does not establish availability.

02 / COMPARE

Measure before choosing a size

Use the item’s measurement chart and compare it with a garment or pair of shoes that fits you. Keep centimetres and inches separate. For an oversized fit, compare actual width and length rather than choosing a larger letter size automatically.

03 / CONFIRM

Check the ordering route

CAFANS is the catalog destination on this page. If you intend to order through PikoBuy, confirm that the original seller URL and item are supported in your current account. Check the quoted charges and current return conditions before paying.

03 / Evidence by category

What should each row include?

Use consistent fields so two similar-looking listings can be compared on the same basis.

CategoryRecord before orderingInspect when photos are available
Shoes & slidesSize system, sizing chart, foot or insole measurement method, selected color.Pair consistency, sole condition, straps, labels and visible damage.
Hoodies & sweatersChest width, shoulder width, body length, sleeve length and listed fabric.Seams, cuffs, print placement and measurements requested for your item.
T-ShirtsGarment width and length, fabric description, fit preference and exact variant.Collar, stitching, embroidery or print details and selected color.
JacketsChest and shoulder measurements, sleeve length, lining and layering allowance.Closures, lining, seams, pockets and visible surface defects.

QC means quality control. Photos can help reveal visible issues, but they do not prove authenticity, composition, durability or fit. Ask specific questions while any applicable return window remains open.

05 / Useful answers

PikoBuy spreadsheet FAQ.

What is a PikoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list intended to help shoppers organize finds before using a shopping agent. Useful entries include source links, exact variants, prices, sizing evidence and notes. This page provides a CAFANS catalog snapshot and comparison guidance; it does not confirm that every item can be purchased through PikoBuy.

Where do the products on this page come from?

The eight displayed records come from the CAFANS public homepage, checked on November 19, 2026. Each card links to its corresponding CAFANS record. The catalog is embedded in this HTML so it remains readable without JavaScript. It does not automatically synchronize with CAFANS.

Do the two main buttons take me to PikoBuy?

No. “PikoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” both open https://cafans.com. They are browsing links to the requested catalog destination and do not create an order or complete payment.

Are the displayed prices the final delivered prices?

No. They are the estimated USD product prices shown by CAFANS at the snapshot date. Selected options, exchange rates, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes may change the total. Confirm the current quote before paying.

Are these products QC verified or authenticated?

No independent QC inspection or authentication has been performed for this directory. Product images are listing images, not evidence of an inspection of your future order. Request current item-specific photos and measurements when that service is available.

What should I do if a product link changes or stops working?

Search the product name or record number on CAFANS and compare the exact variant before continuing. If you cannot identify the same item, remove it from your shortlist rather than substituting a similar photograph without checking the details.

Can I browse without using the filters?

Yes. All eight products and their source links are present in the page. JavaScript adds local search, category filtering, price sorting and the budget calculation; it is not required to read the directory.
